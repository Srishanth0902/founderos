# Decision Framework

## Overview

Whenever I make an important decision, I evaluate it systematically rather than emotionally.

---

# Questions I Ask

Does this solve a real problem?

↓

Will I learn something valuable?

↓

Can this scale?

↓

Is it practical?

↓

Will it still matter in five years?

↓

Does it align with my long-term goals?

---

# Project Selection

I prefer projects that

- teach new skills
- solve real problems
- have business potential
- improve my portfolio
- demonstrate engineering ability

---

# Technology Choice

I choose technologies based on

- ecosystem
- documentation
- maintainability
- scalability
- community

not hype.

---

# Business Thinking

Whenever I see a technology I naturally ask

Can this become

- a product?
- a SaaS?
- an automation?
- an educational platform?

---

# Time Investment

I prefer investing hundreds of hours into one valuable skill rather than learning dozens of unrelated tools.
