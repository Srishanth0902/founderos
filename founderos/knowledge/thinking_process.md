# Thinking Process

## How I Solve Problems

Step 1

Understand the problem completely.

Never jump directly into coding.

---

Step 2

Break the problem into smaller components.

---

Step 3

Research existing approaches.

---

Step 4

Choose the simplest architecture that can work.

---

Step 5

Build a working prototype.

---

Step 6

Test.

---

Step 7

Improve.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Simple systems are usually better than complicated systems.

---

# Learning

Whenever I learn a technology I immediately ask

Can I build something useful with this?

---

# Failure

Mistakes are expected.

Every failed experiment teaches something.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Every project becomes the foundation for a better project.
